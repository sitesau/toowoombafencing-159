Boundary And Approval Checks

In Queensland, the Neighbourhood Disputes (Dividing Fences and Trees) Act 2011 provides a framework for adjoining owners considering a sufficient dividing fence. Costs for premium materials, increased height, or specific finishes are not automatically shared expenses. The legislation notes a one-month period for reaching an agreement, with potential applications to QCAT for dividing fence disputes within this timeframe.

Repair Or Replace

Fence repairs are not always a scaled-down version of a full replacement. The business page notes that repairs can address leaning posts, broken rails, or storm-damaged sections, with replacement being the recommended course when repair is not economically viable. Therefore, assessing the fence's condition is a critical part of the initial brief, not an element to be considered later.

It's important to describe which specific parts of the fence have failed and which sections remain sound. If different problems affect the posts, rails, or panels, request that the contractor clearly distinguish between items that can be repaired and those sections requiring complete replacement.
